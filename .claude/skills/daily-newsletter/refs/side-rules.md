# Rules for side sessions (newsletter, recap and desk)

Everything a research side session needs from the two skill files. Read this instead of SKILL.md; the main run
writes every published word.

**Figures.** Every figure needs its outlet and one independent confirmation; two pages with the same wording are one
source. Where two sources agree within normal rounding, the figure stands; where they disagree, give both with
sources and mark `Open:`. Never guess a figure, and never take one from memory. Give each figure its basis:
consolidated or standalone, quarter or nine months or year, nominal or real, year on year or month on month, and its
date. A close is a closing price, never a live one.

**Company results.** Use profit attributable to shareholders and say so. The percentage change must match the two
figures it compares, and the year-earlier figure comes from the filing or a second outlet, not one report (outlets
sometimes take the wrong base: on November 12, 2026 Mubasher reported +88% for Telecom Egypt's 43.7% rise).

**Sources.** Name the outlet behind a story, not the search result that summarizes it. Yahoo Finance pages marked
Reuters are Reuters. EnterpriseAM and CairoScene are trusted but checked against other outlets where they carry the
story too. Spread world stories across outlets (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, FT, Bloomberg, The National, Asharq
Al-Awsat, Al-Monitor, Al Jazeera); pair Al Jazeera's Gulf politics with a second outlet. Quotes word for word, with
the speaker.

**FactIQ (when connected).** Use it where it holds the answer: Suez, Bab el-Mandeb and Hormuz shipping (IMF
PortWatch, two to three weeks behind: compare with the same weeks a year earlier), Fed, Treasury, BLS, BEA, ECB and
Bank of England figures, central bankers' remarks, US company filings and earnings-call quotes, Brent, gold and FX,
IMF forecasts. It has nothing for Egypt's own markets or monthly data, and its dollar-pound rate is not the central
bank's. Credit the underlying source with its URL, check a figure that moves a story elsewhere too, and log the
lookup as a page (`research_log.py DATE NAME page factiq:<what>`). Missing or signed out: carry on without it.

**Dates and spelling in notes.** US English, month-first dates ("Nov 5"); name the date rather than "yesterday" or
"this week". Proper names keep their own spelling.

**Stop** on a story once each figure has its outlet and one confirmation: about two pages opened beyond search.py's
excerpts, more only where sources conflict.
