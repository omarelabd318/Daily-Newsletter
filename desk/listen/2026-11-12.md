*Markets Desk, Thursday, November 12, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** Telecom Egypt's nine-month profit rose 44% to EGP 24.4bn, and MSCI will add it to its Egypt standard index on November 30 in place of Eastern Company. Elsewedy, e-finance and Emaar Misr also reported; the EGX30 held at 52,500.47 as Arabs sold EGP 141.5m.

**The close:** EGX30 up 0.03% at 52,500.47; EGX70 EWI down 0.26% at 18,447.20; USD/EGP up 0.44% at 53.62; Gold 24K, per gram down 0.57% at 6,990.

**Telecom Egypt's profit rises 44% as MSCI adds it to its Egypt index:** Telecom Egypt's nine-month net profit rose 44% to EGP 24.4bn on revenue up 19% to EGP 93.6bn, with EBITDA up 24% to EGP 43.1bn, a 46% margin, and net debt down to 0.8 times EBITDA from 1.5 times. The desk's view: Index funds tracking MSCI Egypt must buy Telecom Egypt and sell Eastern by November 30, and the results give the buying a fundamental floor: on the last 12 months' profit the stock trades at about 7.7 times earnings.

**Elsewedy, e-finance and Emaar Misr report; growth is uneven:** Elsewedy Electric's nine-month consolidated profit rose 16% to EGP 16.4bn on revenue up 32% to EGP 263bn. e-finance's rose 40% to EGP 2.62bn on revenue up 27% to EGP 6.28bn, and its board proposed about 1.38 free shares per share, subject to the FRA and an extraordinary assembly. The desk's view: e-finance's growth is the cleanest: steady government-payment revenue, with the bonus issue changing only the share count.

**EGX30 flat at 52,500; Arabs sell EGP 141.5m, fertilizers lead:** The EGX30 edged up 0.03% to 52,500.47 on Wednesday on turnover of EGP 11.8bn, against Tuesday's EGP 12.8bn. The desk's view: A flat day on solid turnover is consolidation, not a top: foreigners were net buyers for a second session, and the index sits just under its 50-day average of about 52,777.

**The dollar index hits a 19-month high; the pound eases 0.4%:** The central bank's dollar rate rose to EGP 53.62 on Wednesday from 53.39, a 0.4% fall for the pound, as the dollar index climbed 0.6% to 102.84, its highest since April 2025. The desk's view: The pound's move was the dollar's, not a loss of local support: foreigners bought about $570m of debt on Monday and remittances are at a record.

**The stocks:** Abu Qir led Wednesday, up 5.0%; Eastern rose 4.2% and Banque du Caire fell 2.7%.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: open; The EGX30 holds above 49,255 while foreign institutions keep buying: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Higher rates protect CIB's margins: on track.

**Coming up:** The 182- and 364-day bills today; Cleopatra, Palm Hills and Elsewedy's call Monday.

## United States

**The day:** Nvidia fell 8.6% after hours after a report that its board would decide whether to remove Jensen Huang; the board met on Thursday. Before that, CoreWeave's results lifted the S&P 500 1.31% to 7,619.38, with Oracle up 8.8% and Intel 7.5%.

**The close:** S&P 500 up 1.31% at 7,619.38; Nasdaq up 1.60% at 25,466.19; Dow Jones up 0.49% at 52,486.49.

**Nvidia drops 8.6% after hours as its board weighs removing Huang:** Nvidia rose 2.9% to $173.73 on Wednesday, then fell 8.6% in extended trading after the Wall Street Journal reported that its board would decide whether to remove Jensen Huang, its chief executive for 33 years; the New York Times reported a second affair, with an executive who reports to him. The desk's view: A change at the top is now more likely than not to be decided before results, and either outcome leaves a gap: Huang runs about 60 direct reports with no named successor.

**CoreWeave's results revive the AI trade; Oracle jumps 8.8%:** CoreWeave rose 24.7% after third-quarter revenue rose 168% to $3.66bn and it raised its 2026 revenue outlook to up to $13.3bn, saying it will lean on loans backed by customer contracts rather than unsecured bonds. The desk's view: The rally was relief about funding, which matters most for Oracle, whose debt-funded build-out had cost it a third of its value this year.

**Microsoft falls 2.3% as Anthropic's office tools keep biting:** Microsoft fell 2.3% to $521.13 on Wednesday after Anthropic launched Claude Sheets, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Data, and a filing showed the chief financial officer sold about 7,305 shares at about $544. The desk's view: A second day of losses on the launch says investors take the threat to Office seriously, and Microsoft is also a buyer of Anthropic shares at the IPO price.

**Dollar index reaches 102.84 as Fed hawks eye a half point:** The dollar index rose 0.6% to 102.84 on Wednesday, its highest since April 2025. The desk's view: A quarter point is priced; the debate is now about a half point and more in 2027, which favors cash-rich steady earners over long-dated growth.

**The stocks:** Oracle led Wednesday, up 8.8%; Intel rose 7.5% and Broadcom 4.0%, while Microsoft fell 2.3%.

**The desk's views:** Chip stocks stay under pressure into Apple's results: on track; Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: open; Brent stays below $105 while Iran talks continue: on track.

**Coming up:** Nvidia's board decision; producer prices Friday; Nvidia results November 17.
