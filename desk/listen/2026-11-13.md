*Markets Desk, Friday, November 13, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** Thursday's bill auction drew EGP 282bn of bids for EGP 100bn on offer and the pound firmed to EGP 53.50, even as US 10-year yields hit 5.1%. The EGX30 fell 0.91% to 52,023.37 as blue chips slipped and foreigners sold a net EGP 107.8m; the EGX is shut until Sunday.

**The close:** EGX30 down 0.91% at 52,023.37; EGX70 EWI up 0.43% at 18,527.43; USD/EGP down 0.22% at 53.50; Gold 24K, per gram down 2.72% at 6,800.

**Bills draw EGP 282bn of bids as the pound firms against a 5.1% US yield:** The central bank sold EGP 114.03bn of 182- and 364-day bills on Thursday against a EGP 100bn target, from bids of EGP 282.19bn: the six-month bill yielded 26.07% and the one-year 25.02%. The desk's view: Demand at 2.8 times the amount sold, with yields steady near 25–26%, says the carry trade is holding even as US rates rise: foreign money is still being paid about 12 points over 12.7% inflation.

**EGX30 slips 0.9% as blue chips fall and foreigners sell EGP 108m:** The EGX30 fell 0.91% to 52,023.37 on Thursday, while the EGX70 EWI rose 0.43% to 18,527.43. The desk's view: A 0.9% dip after a 2.3% weekly gain, with locals buying, is profit-taking ahead of the weekend and a jump in US yields, not a turn: the index sits about 1.4% under its 50-day average of about 52,777.

**Brent jumps 3.3% to $95.82 after Trump says no Iran deal is needed:** Brent settled 3.3% higher at $95.82 a barrel on Thursday after President Trump wrote that "We don't need a deal with Iran". The desk's view: The jump undoes most of the week's fall but leaves Brent near $96, well under its 50-day average of about $104 and the desk's $105 line.

**Juhayna's third-quarter profit rises 33%; stock slips 2.3%:** Juhayna's third-quarter revenue rose 25% to a record EGP 10bn and net profit 33% to EGP 654m, after a EGP 107m currency loss; EBITDA margin widened to 14.7% from 14.3% (earnings call, Investing.com transcript). The desk's view: The call confirmed the growth is volume and price, not one-offs, which supports the desk's EGP 27–28 projection by end of January; the fall looks like selling into the news, not a change in the case.

**The stocks:** Juhayna fell 2.3% and Talaat Moustafa 2.0% on Thursday; Alexandria Containers rose 1.7% and Emaar Misr 1.4%.

**The desk's views:** Higher rates protect CIB's margins: on track; The EGX30 holds above 49,255 while foreign institutions keep buying: right; Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: open; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track.

**Coming up:** The EGX reopens Sunday; Palm Hills and Cleopatra report Monday.

## United States

**The day:** Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive on Thursday, with finance chief Colette Kress as interim chief; the stock fell as much as 14.3% before closing 4.3% lower at $166.19. The 10-year yield rose to 5.10% and the S&P 500 slipped 0.35% to 7,592.40, while AMD rose 5.9% and Broadcom 5.5%.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 0.35% at 7,592.40; Nasdaq down 0.39% at 25,366.09; Dow Jones down 0.36% at 52,297.09.

**Huang quits Nvidia; Kress steps in and the stock falls 4.3%:** Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's president, chief executive and director with immediate effect on Thursday, after reports of affairs including one with a woman US authorities suspect of spying for China; finance chief Colette Kress is interim chief while the board considers inside and outside candidates. The desk's view: The removal of uncertainty about whether Huang stays limited the damage: the stock recovered two-thirds of the day's fall.

**10-year yield hits 5.1% as a December hike looks certain:** The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.10% on Thursday and the 2-year to 4.86%, after a hot October CPI and a 3.3% jump in oil. The desk's view: A 5% 10-year makes stocks at 7,600 dearer against bonds and hits housing first.

**Oil jumps 3.3% on Trump's Iran remark; Exxon rises 1.8%:** Brent settled 3.3% higher at $95.82 and WTI 2.6% at $88.74 after President Trump said the US does not need a deal with Iran. The desk's view: The jump keeps oil in a $90–100 range rather than starting a new leg up: US stocks are building and Saudi output is rising.

**Microsoft's Outlook and Teams outage runs into a second day:** Outlook, Teams and other Microsoft 365 services were disrupted from Thursday into Friday; Microsoft ruled out a cyberattack and said early fixes did not work as expected. The desk's view: Outages rarely change a software company's revenue, but a two-day failure feeds the story that hurt the stock this week, Anthropic's office tools.

**The stocks:** Broadcom led Thursday, up 5.5%; Nvidia fell 4.3% and Netflix 2.9%.

**The desk's views:** Chip stocks stay under pressure into Apple's results: on track; Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: open; Brent stays below $105 while Iran talks continue: on track.

**Coming up:** Producer prices today; Nvidia results November 17.
