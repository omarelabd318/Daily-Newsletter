*Markets Desk, Monday, November 16, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** The central bank's dollar rate rose 35 piasters to EGP 53.86 on Sunday, the pound's biggest one-day fall in weeks, even as foreigners kept buying Egyptian debt. Stocks shrugged it off: the EGX30 rose 0.31% and results lifted MOPCO 3.7% and Alexandria Mineral Oils 4.8%.

**The close:** EGX30 up 0.31% at 52,187.20; EGX70 EWI up 1.11% at 18,732.50; USD/EGP up 0.67% at 53.86; Gold 24K, per gram down 0.29% at 6,805.

**Dollar rises 35 piasters to EGP 53.86 despite foreign debt buying:** The central bank's rate closed Sunday at EGP 53.72 to buy and 53.86 to sell, against 53.50 on Thursday, a rise of 35 piasters on the day (central bank, Sada Elbalad, Al-Ain). The desk's view: A dollar at 19-month highs abroad and a US 10-year at 5.15% are pulling on every emerging currency, and the pound is giving back part of its gain rather than breaking: at EGP 53.86 it is still about 1.7% firmer than on November 5.

**MOPCO's nine-month profit jumps 56% to EGP 12.1bn:** MOPCO's net profit after tax rose to EGP 12.14bn in January–September from EGP 7.78bn, on sales up 47% to EGP 27.39bn; gross profit rose 50% to EGP 14.20bn. The desk's view: Urea near $530–540 a ton is paying for the gas cuts, so the desk's view that rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure is being tested by profits, not by supply.

**Palm Hills' profit slips 4.7% as margins shrink:** Palm Hills' nine-month consolidated profit attributable to shareholders fell to EGP 3.37bn from EGP 3.54bn, Youm7 reported from its filing, while revenue rose 24.4% to EGP 31.80bn; the gross margin fell to 35.1% from 40.8%. The desk's view: Both big developers are selling more and keeping less: costs and financing at 25% bill yields eat the gain from higher prices.

**Edita's third-quarter profit rises 41.5% on record sales:** Edita's third-quarter net profit rose 41.5% to EGP 938.1m on record revenue of EGP 7.3bn, up 32.9%; the gross margin fell to 32.9% from 35%. The desk's view: Volume and price both grew while the margin held near 33%, the kind of quarter that keeps food producers among the desk's preferred sectors after Juhayna's 33% profit rise.

**The stocks:** Results lifted MOPCO 3.7% and Alexandria Mineral Oils 4.8% on Sunday: MOPCO's nine-month profit rose 56% to EGP 12.14bn, and Alexandria Mineral Oils' consolidated profit to EGP 3.47bn from EGP 1.37bn.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Gas rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure through November: open; Developers lag while rates stay high: open.

**Coming up:** Egypt Aluminium goes ex-dividend Tuesday; Nvidia reports Tuesday; EFG Holding Wednesday.

## United States

**The day:** Asian chip stocks slumped on Monday, the Taiex more than 4%, and Nasdaq 100 futures fell 0.5% a day before Nvidia's first results since Jensen Huang's exit. Nvidia has lost about $1.9tn of market value since early October.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 0.40% at 7,561.67; Nasdaq down 0.91% at 25,136.27; Dow Jones up 0.10% at 52,347.55.

**Chip rout spreads to Asia a day before Nvidia's results:** Taiwan's Taiex fell more than 4% on Monday and Nasdaq 100 futures 0.5%, after the cash index lost 1.2% on Friday, when AMD fell 8.8% and Broadcom 4.8%. The desk's view: Tuesday is the week's only real test: at about 12 times expected earnings Nvidia is priced for disappointment, so guidance at or above $108bn and a clear word on the chief executive could set off a sharp rebound in the chip names.

**December hike odds hold near 89% after softer producer prices:** Producer prices rose 0.3% in October against 0.4% expected, and 6.5% from a year earlier, the most since November 2022. The desk's view: A hike in December is priced; the question is whether it is one and done.

**Bitcoin hits $62,006, its lowest since July:** Bitcoin fell to $62,006 on Sunday on Binance, its lowest since July, and traded near $64,355 on Monday morning, about 29% below its September high of $87,364. The desk's view: Bitcoin is trading as the riskiest end of the same trade that is hurting chip stocks: dearer money and a stronger dollar.

**Brent slips to $95.30 on surplus forecasts:** Brent fell 54 cents to $95.30 early on Monday and WTI 0.3% to $88.34, after the IEA said on Friday that the world market returns to surplus in the fourth quarter and China's October refinery runs fell. The desk's view: With a surplus forecast and Gulf supply finding other routes, the desk keeps its view that Brent stays below $105 into November 20, raising its confidence to medium with four sessions to go.

**The stocks:** Broadcom fell 4.8% to $347.01 and Nvidia 2.0% to $162.85 on Friday, and Nasdaq 100 futures fell 0.5% on Monday as Asian chip stocks slid; Nvidia reports Tuesday after the close.

**The desk's views:** Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: on track; Brent stays below $105 while Iran talks continue: on track.

**Coming up:** Nvidia results Tuesday after the close; Fed minutes Wednesday; Walmart Thursday.
