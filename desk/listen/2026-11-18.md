*Markets Desk, Wednesday, November 18, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** Arabs and foreigners bought a net EGP 195m of Egyptian shares on Tuesday while locals sold, as the EGX30 fell 0.99% to 51,681.60 and 179 stocks dropped. Fertilizer makers now pay 50% more for gas, and Orascom Construction's nine-month profit nearly doubled.

**The close:** EGX30 down 0.99% at 51,681.60; EGX70 EWI down 2.42% at 17,592.80; USD/EGP up 0.09% at 53.80; Gold 24K, per gram down 0.15% at 6,745.

**Outside money buys EGX shares as locals sell into a 0.99% fall:** The EGX30 fell 0.99% to 51,681.60 on Tuesday and the EGX70 EWI 2.42% to 17,592.80, as 179 stocks fell and 31 rose on EGP 9.42bn of turnover. The desk's view: The selling was local and broad, after a 7.7% small-cap run in the week to November 12, not an exit by outside money: Arabs and foreigners were net buyers on a day every S&P sector fell.

**Fertilizer gas costs 50% more, in return for full exports:** Seven fertilizer producers agreed to pay $175m upfront for gas at $12.75 per mmBtu, up 50% from $8.50, for 45 days from November 16, in return for exporting all their output and a pause in supplying subsidized fertilizer; gas cuts of 20–40% stay. The desk's view: At about 25 mmBtu a ton, the rise adds roughly $100 to the gas cost of each ton of urea, close to a fifth of its export price, so margins fall even though volumes recover.

**Orascom Construction's nine-month profit nearly doubles to $218m:** Profit attributable to shareholders rose 95.9% to $218.0m in the nine months to September against an adjusted $111.3m, which leaves out a $22.0m gain from legal cases; on the reported base the rise is 63.5%. The desk's view: Dollar earnings growing this fast in both the Gulf and the US put the stock at about 6 times earnings, cheap for a firm whose revenue is mostly in dollars.

**EFG Holding's quarterly profit falls 15% as its lender lags:** Profit after tax and minorities fell 15% to EGP 720m in the third quarter as revenue rose 5% to EGP 6.7bn, or 27% excluding one-off asset-sale gains at Banque Nextt a year earlier. The desk's view: The investment bank is earning from the busiest EGX turnover in a year and the state's offerings, and it runs Misr Life's 15% sale in December; the lending arm and higher taxes are the drag.

**The stocks:** Palm Hills fell 3.5% to EGP 11.44 and Talaat Moustafa 1.8% to EGP 82.38 as 179 stocks fell; Egypt Aluminium's 5.2% drop to EGP 310.07 was almost all its EGP 16 dividend.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Gas rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure through November: on track; Developers lag while rates stay high: on track.

**Coming up:** Fed minutes Wednesday night; Orascom Construction's new shares list November 24; MSCI changes November 30.

## United States

**The day:** Nvidia's revenue rose 103% to $115.8bn, above the $109.1bn expected, and it guided to about $130bn, lifting its shares about 3.5% after hours. That came after the S&P 500's fourth straight fall, to its lowest close since July, led by software.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 1.03% at 7,340.53; Nasdaq down 0.74% at 24,328.38; Dow Jones down 1.64% at 50,759.42.

**Nvidia beats with $115.8bn and guides to $130bn:** Nvidia's revenue rose 103% to $115.8bn in the quarter to October 25, against $109.1bn expected, with data-center revenue up 110% to $107.6bn; GAAP net income rose 105% to $65.3bn. The desk's view: The beat settles the demand question for another quarter, and at RSI 23 before the report the stock was washed out, so a relief rally in chips is likely.

**Software leads a fourth S&P 500 fall as the Dow loses 849 points:** The S&P 500 fell 1.03% to 7,340.53, its lowest close since late July, the Dow 1.64% to 50,759.42 and the Nasdaq 0.74%; every S&P sector fell and the VIX rose 11.1% to 25.46. The desk's view: The fear of AI has moved from those spending on it to those it could replace: software sold off hardest on the eve of a listing that will raise up to $64.4bn for an AI developer.

**Yields above 5% and a priced December hike push gold below $3,800:** Spot gold fell below $3,800 on Wednesday for the first time since September 30, 2025, as the dollar index stood near its highest since April 2025 and the 10-year Treasury yield held above 5%. The desk's view: Real yields this high are the main weight on gold and on long-duration stocks; the minutes can only add to it if they show more officials wanted to hike in October.

**Anthropic prices a listing of up to $64.4bn after the close:** Anthropic prices 825 million shares at $70–78 after the US close on Wednesday, which would raise $57.8bn to $64.4bn; it said in a November 17 filing it remains on track for that range. The desk's view: A deal this size draws money from other AI and software stocks in the days around it, which fits Tuesday's software selling; a strong first trade would also set a value for the AI developers that Alphabet and others hold stakes in.

**The stocks:** Oracle fell 3.4% to $115.00, JPMorgan 2.4% to $329.96 and Microsoft 2.3% to $506.24 as software led the sell-off.

**The desk's views:** Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: on track; Brent stays below $105 while Iran talks continue: on track.

**Coming up:** Fed minutes and Anthropic's pricing Wednesday; Walmart results and Anthropic's first trade Thursday.
