*Markets Desk, Thursday, November 19, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** The IMF's staff deal on Egypt's final review, worth up to $2.3bn, lands with the pound at EGP 53.89 and the EGX30 drifting on its thinnest turnover in a year. The index slipped 0.26% to 51,544.80 on EGP 6.7bn traded, while EFG Holding jumped 5.0% after its results.

**The close:** EGX30 down 0.26% at 51,544.80; EGX70 EWI down 0.16% at 17,564.30; USD/EGP up 0.16% at 53.89; Gold 24K, per gram down 2.59% at 6,570.

**IMF staff deal on the final review unlocks up to $2.3bn:** The IMF and Egypt reached a staff-level agreement on the eighth and final review of the loan program, which could release up to $2.3bn once the IMF board approves it, before the program expires on December 15. The desk's view: The deal removes the main local risk to the pound before the Fed's December 9 decision: it keeps foreign money in T-bills even as 5% US yields shrink the carry.

**EGX30 slips 0.26% on its thinnest turnover in a year:** The EGX30 fell 0.26% to 51,544.80 on Wednesday and the EGX70 EWI 0.16% to 17,564.30. The desk's view: A market waiting, not selling: there is little local news to sell on, but buyers are holding back until the IMF board vote and MSCI's November 30 changes.

**EFG Holding jumps 5% as its investment bank carries the quarter:** EFG Holding rose 5.0% to EGP 25.30 on about six times Tuesday's volume. The desk's view: The market looked through the headline fall to a recovering investment bank, which earns more as turnover and listings pick up after the IMF deal.

**Orascom Construction doubles quarterly profit on US data centers:** Orascom Construction's third-quarter profit attributable to shareholders rose 103% to $102.7m on revenue up 22.5% to $1.8bn, and US data-center work lifted its backlog to a record $11.6bn, excluding BESIX. The desk's view: Earnings now rest on US data centers as much as Egypt and the Gulf, which the EGX price does not fully reflect.

**The stocks:** EFG Holding rose 5.0% to EGP 25.30 on about six times Tuesday's volume after its results; Elsewedy fell 1.5% to EGP 117.97.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Gas rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure through November: on track; Developers lag while rates stay high: on track.

**Coming up:** Walmart results and Anthropic's first trade Thursday; Orascom Construction's new shares list November 24.

## United States

**The day:** Anthropic priced its listing at $83, above its range, to raise a record $81.3bn, and Nasdaq futures rose more than 1% on Thursday morning. On Wednesday the S&P 500 fell for a fifth day as health insurers sank, while cyber stocks and Amazon rallied.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 0.23% at 7,323.63; Nasdaq up 0.42% at 24,429.89; Dow Jones down 1.11% at 50,198.17.

**Anthropic prices at $83, raising a record $81.3bn:** Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83, above its $70–78 range, raising about $81.3bn before fees at a valuation near $1.5tn, the largest listing on record. The desk's view: Demand above the range shows investors still pay up for the companies building AI models, even as they sell the software those models could replace.

**Medicare audit plan sinks Humana 13% and UnitedHealth 10%:** The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services proposed tighter anti-fraud audits and oversight of Medicare and Medicaid plans, raising fears of payments being clawed back. The desk's view: A proposed rule with a comment period ahead leaves the size of the hit unknown, and uncertainty is what the sector is being priced for.

**Palo Alto's 39% revenue rise and $4.5bn deal lift cyber stocks:** Palo Alto Networks' quarterly revenue rose 39% to $3.44bn, with adjusted earnings of $1.05 a share, and it agreed to buy Applied Compute for $4.5bn in stock. The desk's view: AI raises the threat and the budget: security is the part of software that gains from AI rather than being replaced by it.

**Fed minutes back a December hike as the dollar hits a 19-month high:** Minutes of the Fed's October 27–28 meeting showed some officials favored a rate increase then, and a couple floated more than a quarter point in December. The desk's view: Rates go up, not down, through the year-end, and a half point is now a real risk.

**The stocks:** Amazon rose 3.8% to $267.74 and Broadcom 3.6% to $354.05, while Exxon Mobil fell 4.0% to $160.58 and Nvidia 2.9% to $150.96, its lowest since June 2025.

**The desk's views:** Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: on track; Brent stays below $105 while Iran talks continue: on track.

**Coming up:** Walmart's results and jobless claims Thursday afternoon; Anthropic's first trade after the open.
