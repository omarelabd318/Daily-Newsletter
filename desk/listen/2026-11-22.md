*Markets Desk, Sunday, November 22, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** Goldman Sachs expects the central bank to hold its 20% deposit rate until late in the first quarter, then cut 700 basis points by 2028, which keeps Egyptian bills' carry intact while 10-year Treasuries sit near 5%. The EGX reopens on Sunday after the S&P 500's 3.7% fall on Friday.

**The close:** EGX30 down 0.17% at 51,458.00; EGX70 EWI down 0.33% at 17,505.80; USD/EGP up 0.16% at 53.88; Gold 24K, per gram down 3.78% at 6,240.

**Goldman sees rates held at 20% until late in the first quarter:** Goldman Sachs expects the central bank to keep its overnight deposit rate at 20% until late in the first quarter of 2027, then cut 500 basis points in 2027 and 200 in 2028, to 13%, EnterpriseAM reported from the bank's note, which Goldman's Farouk Soussa confirmed to it. The desk's view: A hold into the first quarter keeps the one-year T-bill near 26% and the carry wide enough to hold foreign money even with the Fed expected to hike on December 9, which is what the pound needs into the IMF program's December 15 expiry.

**Foreigners sell and Fed-hike odds hit 97%; EGX30 drifts on thin trade:** The EGX30 fell 0.17% to 51,458.04 on Thursday and the EGX70 EWI 0.33% to 17,505.78, with 125 stocks down and 75 up, on EGP 6.24bn of turnover, the lowest in a year in the data log. The desk's view: A small, orderly foreign retreat, not an exit: with the US 10-year at 5.04%, the carry on Egyptian bills is narrower, and foreigners trim stocks first.

**Misr Life readies a 15% listing in December, the EGX's biggest test:** Misr Life Insurance plans to sell 750 million existing shares, 15% of its capital, with trading targeted for early December and the prospectus due for approval early this week; retail investors get 5% to 10% of the offer and institutions the rest. The desk's view: A state sale of this size draws cash from the rest of the market in the weeks around the book, as Banque du Caire's did on October 29, when turnover already sits about 44% below its 90-day average.

**Weatherford hired to restart about 100 idle oil wells within 18 months:** The Egyptian General Petroleum Corporation signed a framework agreement with Weatherford on Friday to bring about 100 shut-in wells, onshore and offshore, back into production within 18 months, using a no-rig fix chosen well by well. The desk's view: Each barrel produced at home is a barrel the state need not import at $92 Brent, so the program helps the fuel bill and the pound at the margin.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Gas rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure through November: on track; Developers lag while rates stay high: on track.

**Coming up:** Cleopatra Hospital's third-quarter results are expected Monday and Orascom Construction's new shares list Tuesday; Misr Life's prospectus approval is due this week.

## United States

**The day:** Nvidia fell 14% on a report of an SEC accounting probe and the S&P 500 3.7% to 6,910.39, ending its worst week since 2020 in correction. Investors fled to Treasuries, pulling the 10-year yield to 4.92%, and the VIX closed at 35.89.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 3.70% at 6,910.39; Nasdaq down 5.28% at 22,617.73; Dow Jones down 1.93% at 48,147.33.

**SEC probe report sinks Nvidia 14%; S&P 500's worst week since 2020:** The S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39 on Friday, the Nasdaq 5.28% to 22,617.73 and the Dow 1.93% to 48,147.33; the S&P 500 and the Dow closed in correction, the S&P's seventh straight fall was its longest since February 2020, and the Nasdaq erased its gain for the year. The desk's view: The report turns a valuation reset into a question about the quality of AI earnings, which is harder to buy on a dip: until Nvidia or the SEC says more, the desk expects the AI leaders to keep lagging steady earners into November 27, its open rotation view.

**Broadcom drops 12% on a report Google trims 2027 TPU orders:** Broadcom fell 12.2% to $304.41 after analyst Ming-Chi Kuo said Google is shifting part of its planned 2027 TPU orders to MediaTek (24/7 Wall St, MLQ); TipRanks put the cut at about 20%. The desk's view: A survey-based analyst note is not an order book, but it hits the custom-chip case at its weakest moment, with buyers already questioning AI budgets.

**Investors buy Treasuries: the 10-year falls to 4.92%, the VIX to 35.89:** The 10-year Treasury yield fell to 4.92% on Friday from 5.04% on Thursday, about 20 basis points lower on the week as the AI selloff drove money into government debt, and the VIX closed at 35.89. The desk's view: Lower yields here come from fear, not from a softer Fed: the hike is still priced.

**Global Payments jumps 9.9% on a report GTCR weighs a $50bn buyout:** Global Payments rose 9.95% to $91.83 after a report that GTCR, its largest shareholder with about 16%, is weighing a full takeover valued at about $50bn including debt; the stock had been up about 14% during the session. The desk's view: A private-equity bid in a week like this shows buyers paying for steady cash flows the market is selling with everything else, which fits the desk's steady-earner case.

**The desk's views:** Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: on track.

**Coming up:** A short week: PCE inflation and GDP on Wednesday, then markets close Thursday for Thanksgiving.
