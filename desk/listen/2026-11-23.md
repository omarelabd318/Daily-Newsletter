*Markets Desk, Monday, November 23, 2026.*

## Egypt

**The day:** The dollar jumped 63 piastres to EGP 54.50 and the EGX30 fell 2.85% to 49,989.80 as Wall Street's rout reached Cairo, but the selling was local: Egyptian institutions sold a net EGP 181m while foreign institutions bought EGP 170m. Bills clearing at 26.29% say rates stay high.

**The close:** EGX30 down 2.85% at 49,989.80; EGX70 EWI down 5.55% at 16,534.40; USD/EGP up 1.16% at 54.50; Gold 24K, per gram up 2.00% at 6,365.

**Pound slips to 54.50 and EGX30 breaks 50,000 on local selling:** The central bank's selling rate rose 63 piastres to EGP 54.50 from 53.87 at Thursday's close, and the EGX30 fell 2.85% to 49,989.79, its fourth straight loss, while the EGX70 EWI lost 5.55%; 205 stocks fell and 4 rose on EGP 6.6bn of turnover. The desk's view: A leveraged, local sell-off rather than foreign flight: foreign institutions bought, and Al-Borsa puts margin loans near EGP 17bn, so forced selling can run a session or two before it burns out.

**Bills clear at 26.29% and the state weighs a 19.5% sukuk:** At Sunday's auction the central bank sold 273-day bills at 26.29%, accepting EGP 104bn against an EGP 80bn target, and 91-day bills at 24.92%, EGP 140.5bn in all. The desk's view: Demand is there, but at a price: the state taking more than it planned at above 26% says it needs the money and will pay for it, which makes a cut on December 17 unlikely with a Fed hike due on December 9.

**Three LNG cargoes turn away from Egypt as Europe pays more:** Three LNG tankers bound for Egyptian terminals changed course in recent days, two toward Europe, where buyers pay more, EnterpriseAM reported from Bloomberg ship-tracking data compiled with Kpler. The desk's view: Each lost cargo makes it harder to restore the gas cut from fertilizer plants, whose gas already costs 50% more, $12.75 per mmBtu, until December 31; the desk keeps its view that the sector stays under pressure through November.

**Misr Life valued at EGP 69.1bn ahead of a December sale:** A government committee valued Misr Life Insurance at about EGP 69.1bn, EGP 13.82 a share, an unnamed official told Asharq Business, and the company confirmed it plans to sell 15%, 750 million existing shares, in December. The desk's view: In a market that lost about EGP 142bn of value in a day and leans on margin, a EGP 10bn state sale draws cash from mid-caps until it prices; a well-covered book would show buyers are still there.

**The desk's views:** Telecom Egypt holds its ground into MSCI's November 30 inclusion: on track; The pound stays firmer than EGP 56 per dollar into the IMF review: on track; Gas rationing keeps fertilizer stocks under pressure through November: on track; Developers lag while rates stay high: on track.

**Coming up:** The EGX trades Monday after Sunday's 2.85% fall; Orascom Construction's new shares list Tuesday.

## United States

**The day:** Nvidia fell 14% and the S&P 500 3.7% to 6,910.39 on Friday, ending Wall Street's worst week since 2020. On Monday Nasdaq 100 futures rose about 2% even as South Korea's KOSPI closed 3.3% lower; PCE inflation and GDP come Wednesday.

**The close:** S&P 500 down 3.70% at 6,910.39; Nasdaq down 5.28% at 22,617.73; Dow Jones down 1.93% at 48,147.33.

**SEC probe report sinks Nvidia 14%; S&P 500's worst week since 2020:** The S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39 on Friday, the Nasdaq 5.28% to 22,617.73 and the Dow 1.93% to 48,147.33; the S&P 500 and the Dow closed in correction, the S&P's seventh straight fall was its longest since February 2020, and the Nasdaq erased its gain for the year. The desk's view: The report turns a valuation reset into a question about the quality of AI earnings, which is harder to buy on a dip: until Nvidia or the SEC says more, the desk expects the AI leaders to keep lagging steady earners into November 27, its open rotation view.

**Broadcom drops 12% on a report Google trims 2027 TPU orders:** Broadcom fell 12.2% to $304.41 after analyst Ming-Chi Kuo said Google is shifting part of its planned 2027 TPU orders to MediaTek (24/7 Wall St, MLQ); TipRanks put the cut at about 20%. The desk's view: A survey-based analyst note is not an order book, but it hits the custom-chip case at its weakest moment, with buyers already questioning AI budgets.

**Investors buy Treasuries: the 10-year falls to 4.92%, the VIX to 35.89:** The 10-year Treasury yield fell to 4.92% on Friday from 5.04% on Thursday, about 20 basis points lower on the week as the AI selloff drove money into government debt, and the VIX closed at 35.89. The desk's view: Lower yields here come from fear, not from a softer Fed: the hike is still priced.

**Global Payments jumps 9.9% on a report GTCR weighs a $50bn buyout:** Global Payments rose 9.95% to $91.83 after a report that GTCR, its largest shareholder with about 16%, is weighing a full takeover valued at about $50bn including debt; the stock had been up about 14% during the session. The desk's view: A private-equity bid in a week like this shows buyers paying for steady cash flows the market is selling with everything else, which fits the desk's steady-earner case.

**The desk's views:** Money rotates from AI spenders to steady earners: on track.

**Coming up:** A short week: PCE inflation and GDP on Wednesday, then markets close Thursday for Thanksgiving.
