### Egypt first country outside Europe aligned with EU e-signature rules
- Administrative arrangement signed Nov 13 by ITIDA and the European Commission; Communications Minister Raafat Hindy attended by video.
- Commission plans to add Egypt to its third-country list of trusted lists (with Moldova, Ukraine), so EU states can validate Egyptian e-signatures and seals; ITIDA adds the EU list in return.
- EU: 24.6% of Egypt's total trade, EUR 32.3bn of goods trade in 2025 (EnterpriseAM, citing EU data; single source).
- A deliverable of the EU-Egypt summit of Oct 22, 2025 (Zawya release).
- Src: EnterpriseAM (AM edition, above link); Egypt Independent https://www.egyptindependent.com/egypt-becomes-first-non-european-country-to-unlock-eu-digital-signature-trust/
