### Starship ship caught by tower arms after orbit; booster lost
- Nov 14: SpaceX's Starship upper stage returned from orbit and was caught by the "Mechazilla" arms at Starbase, Texas, the first capture of a ship returning from space. Launched late Nov 13; 22 hours, 14 laps; released 26 Starlink satellites; 15th full-scale flight.
- Super Heavy booster failed its return: engines cut out early, it self-destructed in the Gulf, so no double catch. Three earlier booster catches stand.
- Quote: "Can you believe it? ... Look at her! Baby, she's back!" (SpaceX commentator Dan Huot). NASA's Jared Isaacman: "It was almost magic."

### Italy fines Meta EUR 805m over Android photo access
- Nov 14: Italy's data protection authority fined Meta EUR 805m ($887m), about 0.4% of annual turnover; largest it has imposed on a big tech firm (DW).

- Meta has 60 days to switch it off and seek separate consent. Meta will appeal: "we strongly disagree" and calls the fine "disproportionate".

## Health and Science

### Brain implants let ALS patients speak and sing
- UC Davis team, to be presented at the Society for Neuroscience meeting in Washington. Casey Harrell (joined 2023 at 45; four arrays, 256 electrodes) used the system for nearly 2m words over two years. Reanalysis of the same data raised intelligibility by as much as 94% (preprint; listeners had transcribed only a little over half of the words in the 2025 Nature work).
- A woman (T21, 384 electrodes, trained singer) could sing a musical scale and convey questioning or joyful tone; results preliminary, unpublished. Needs live testing; users still connect by skull ports.
- Quote: "This system, for the first time, is good enough ... Good enough for real communication. It's good enough to sing." (Cindy Chestek, University of Michigan, not involved).
- Src: NYT https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/14/science/brain-implants-als-voice.html (read via SSBCrack copy of its text, https://news.ssbcrack.com/brain-implants-enable-real-time-speech-and-singing-for-people-with-als/)
