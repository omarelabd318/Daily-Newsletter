- Bahrain Interior Ministry issued two threat alerts Sunday, first since September; first nationwide in months; residents told to go to nearest safe place. Blasts about 4 a.m. and 6:30 a.m. local (AFP reporter).

- War nearly 9 months old (began Feb 28); April ceasefire; US-Iran deal talks uncertain.
