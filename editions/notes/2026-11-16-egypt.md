### Private-sector minimum wage to EGP 8,500 from Jan 1, 2027
- National Wages Council approved Nov 15, extraordinary meeting chaired by PM Mostafa Madbouly; up from EGP 7,000 (+21%, first rise since March 2025 per EnterpriseAM)
- Part-time/temporary minimum net hourly wage EGP 34; minimum annual raise 3% of social insurance wage, at least EGP 350, from 2027
- EnterpriseAM: 26.5mn private-sector workers; public-sector floor EGP 8,000 since July; target EGP 15,000 public floor by FY2029/30
- Src: Arab Finance https://www.arabfinance.com/en/news/newdetails/egypt-raises-minimum-wage-for-private-sector-from-January-2027
- Chk: CairoScene https://cairoscene.com/News/Private-Sector-Minimum-Wage-to-Rise-to-EGP-8-500-in-January-2027 (7,000 to 8,500, EGP 34); EnterpriseAM https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/11/16/raising-the-wage-floor/
- Why: pay floor for most of the workforce, inflation 12.7%
- Open: 26.5mn workers and "first since March 2025" are EnterpriseAM only
