## Regional and Gulf

### Houthis advance toward Aden as Yemeni government forces pull back from Bab el-Mandeb
- Sun Nov 15: government-aligned forces (Saudi-backed) said they withdrew from Dhubab airport and ground near Bab el-Mandeb, "redeploying" to defend Aden, the interim capital, after weeks of fighting; Houthis left in control of the area overlooking the strait (NBC, Al Jazeera, BBC).
- AFP via DW (Nov 15): Houthis about 60 km (under 40 miles) from Aden. UN: nearly 330,000 displaced since fighting escalated in August (BBC; other Nov 15 notes say 340,000+ IOM: figures differ). BBC: over 60% of documented civilian casualties since August were women and children.
- NBC: gives Houthis "greater leverage" over the choke point while Iran disrupts Hormuz; fighting broke a 2022 UN truce.

- Why: Bab el-Mandeb is the southern gate of the Red Sea and Suez Canal route.

- NYT: "dozens" sentenced to life; seen as rising sectarian tension amid the war on Iran.
