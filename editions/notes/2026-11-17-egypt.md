### Electricity ministry studies 3-4 new models for household tariff slabs
- Minister Mahmoud Esmat to House energy and environment committee (chair Tarek El Molla), Mon Nov 16
- 3 or 4 models: re-split slabs, add new slabs, or keep system with new cut-offs. No date, no decision
- Problem: going from 649 to 651 kWh can add about EGP 400 to a bill
- Earlier model (0-50, 0-100 kWh slabs, each kWh at its own slab's tariff) would have saved ministry about EGP 17bn without price rises but raised bills for 300-400 kWh users
- Finance Ministry to pay over EGP 260bn owed to Petroleum Ministry; subsidy dues not paid 2021-2023
- Ministry pays about EGP 13bn/month of a EGP 24-25bn monthly gas bill; Finance covers about EGP 10bn/month
- Dollar from EGP 50 to 55 added about EGP 2.5bn to the petroleum bill
- Separate, Nov 16 (DNE): Madbouly meeting on saving incentives; target 1,000 MW of solar plants for about 7,000 factories
