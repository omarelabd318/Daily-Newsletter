### Parliament passes electricity law amendments: fines up to EGP 1m
- Nov 16: House (Speaker Hisham Badawi) gave final approval to the government's amendments to Electricity Law 87/2015: energy efficiency, licensing of energy-efficiency firms, wider powers for the Electricity Regulatory Agency (EgyptERA), including a new monitoring unit
- Art. 75 (energy-efficiency obligations): fine up to EGP 500k, doubled for a repeat offense
- Art. 76: breach of Art. 51 (producers' and importers' duty to put energy-efficiency labels on appliances): EGP 200k–1m plus confiscation of the equipment
- MP Rasha Ramadan (deputy head, energy committee): the fines target licensed firms, not citizens; efficiency measures at home are optional
- Src: Youm7, https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/16/لترشيد-الاستهلاك-وخفض-الفاقد-مجلس-النواب-يقر-نهائيا-تعديلات-قانون/7620872 (final approval, aims)
