### IMF staff-level agreement on final review, up to $2.3bn
- IMF statement Nov 18: staff-level agreement on 8th and final review of 48-month EFF and 3rd review of RSF; needs Executive Board approval.
- EFF: SDR1,113.02mn (~$1.5bn). RSF: up to SDR600mn (~$0.8bn). Total ~$2.3bn. Total disbursements under both would reach SDR7,111.69mn (~$9.6bn). Program entered Dec 2022, expands to $8bn (Mar 2024), expires Dec 15. Board date not announced.
- Mission chief Amine Mati: "exchange-rate flexibility should remain the first line of defense against external shocks, with interventions limited to containing excessive volatility." Welcomed CBE's 100bp hike in October; downside risks "significant"; inflation "still elevated" but better than expected. Wants timely fuel price adjustments, EGPC viability plan, state-entity competitive neutrality (Future of Egypt).
- Divestment: ~$1.2bn booked (EnterpriseAM's math: $526.3mn by July + Banque du Caire $658mn) vs ~$1.5bn IMF wants; Misr Life 15% float (750mn shares) in December.
- Reconcile: $1.5bn = EFF, $0.8bn = RSF; "$1.5bn" outlets cite only the EFF part.
- Maait (IMF director for Arab group) expects board pass; govt not keen on successor program (EnterpriseAM).
