### Hundreds of coffins found at Kom Abu Billo, Menoufia
- Supreme Council of Antiquities mission found a cemetery of "hundreds" of stone and pottery coffins, with wooden coffin remains, domed mudbrick tombs and a limestone-lined tomb (announced Nov 18). Preliminary study: used mainly by the middle class; much dates to the 3rd and 2nd centuries BC (Anadolu). Also "gold tongues" (Anadolu headline). Sada El Balad: period from end of Late Period through Ptolemaic to early Roman. No exact count found.
