### ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- Thu Nov 19: International Court of Justice found Myanmar responsible for genocide in the 2016-17 "clearance operations", in the case Gambia brought in Nov 2019; first time the ICJ has found a state responsible for genocide (HRW, Al Jazeera, Guardian). Vote 9 to 6 (HRW); final, no appeal. Court found about 10,000 Rohingya killed (Guardian); more than 700,000 fled to Bangladesh in 2017 (HRW); about 1.2 million refugees now in Bangladesh (Guardian).
- Orders: cease violations, assurances of non-repetition, reparations including safe return. US State Department said it was examining the judgment, stressing avoiding "politicisation"; EU: "compliance with ICJ's decisions is not optional" (Guardian). UK expelled Myanmar's defence attache.
