### IMF staff-level deal on final review; board date still not set

### Private-sector minimum wage rises to EGP 8,500 from Jan 1, 2027
- National Wages Council (chair PM Mostafa Madbouly) approved Nov 15: EGP 8,500 a month from EGP 7,000 (+21%); minimum annual raise 3% of insured wage, at least EGP 350; part-time hourly minimum EGP 34 net.
- SME rep Alaa El-Sakkaty: the 8,500 includes in-kind benefits; average cash about EGP 6,500 (Al-Masry Al-Youm, Nov 18). Labor Ministry: intensive inspections from January.
