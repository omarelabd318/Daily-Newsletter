### Iran war: Gulf drone claims, Senate vote, and no talks
- Iranian claims: IRGC said Sun Nov 15 six drones hit a US air-defense radar at Riffa air base, Bahrain; Bahrain issued its first nationwide alerts in months and said it intercepted several attacks. Tue Nov 17: IRGC claimed another drone strike on a US radar at Sheikh Isa air base, Bahrain; Centcom said drones intercepted, no damage or injuries. Wed Nov 18: Iran's army said it hit US Patriot systems and launch platforms at Ali Al Salem base, Kuwait; Kuwait's army said it countered "hostile drone attacks" and named no base. Iranian claims, no damage shown.
- Tue Nov 17: US jets struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island, "in response to Iranian aggression" (US military official).
- Thu Nov 19: Senate rejected the House-passed Iran war-powers resolution 51-48; Collins, Murkowski and Paul voted yes, Fetterman was the only Democrat voting no (CBS). Largely symbolic. Sen. Tim Kaine: "its unpopularity was made absolutely manifestly clear in the election results from Nov. 3."
- Thu Nov 19: Trump on Truth Social: "For Iran, the Clock is Ticking, and they better get moving, FAST, or there won't be anything left of them." US Embassy Kuwait told Americans region-wide to stay alert, expect higher Houthi and Iranian threat, prepare for airspace closures (topics.json, Nov 20 edition; not rechecked).
- Talks stalled: June 18 US-Iran memorandum stalled over Hormuz (Shorouk); Araghchi left Muscat Nov 17 with no deal; Sat Nov 21 Araghchi said the "no war, no peace" standoff with Washington had to end (DNE Africa).

### Houthis push toward Aden; Saudi Arabia hit and replaces commander; Egypt's troops

- Now: Houthis within about 80 km of Aden from the north and 110 km from the west (Reuters, via Geo News, Fri Nov 20); government forces digging in with checkpoints and trenches (Crypto Briefing, citing reports; Straits Times headline). Earlier reports said 60 km north (AFP). Maritime Executive: Houthis consolidated Ras Menhali peninsula and Perim Island overlooking Bab el-Mandeb.
- Thu Nov 19: Houthi missile damaged the 80-storey PIF Tower in Riyadh (Saudi statement "material damage but no injuries"); second Riyadh landmark hit this month (Aramco building Nov 8). Coalition intercepted further missiles over Riyadh the same day (Kuwait Times: four).
- Fri Nov 20: coalition said it struck Hodeidah port; Houthis threatened Saudi Red Sea ports, infrastructure and airspace (Geo News, Al Jazeera).
- Egypt: FM Badr Abdelatty (Paris, Nov 17): Egyptian forces in Saudi Arabia "for several months", strictly defensive, "will not enter Yemen"; parliament approved a mandate by two-thirds (CairoScene).

Third attack in a week on Nov 19; Crimea governor Aksyonov: "As a result of another attack on the Crimean Bridge, traffic has been temporarily suspended" (CNN). ### Russian missiles crash in Poland; Europe reacts
- Fri Nov 20 (overnight attack on Ukraine): two Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone fell on Polish territory, at Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem and Molodycz, said Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz (RMF24 via Kyiv Independent). One landed about 35 km from where Western military exercises were under way (AP); the Kh-101 went down about 50 km from the Ukraine border, the drone about 30 km.
- PM Donald Tusk: did not appear to be an accident while exercises were under way (BBC). NATO's Mark Rutte: "grave violation" of Polish airspace. European leaders called it a "provocation" and an "irresponsible gamble" (Guardian). Poland did not invoke Article 4 (Latin Times correction); it asked NATO for more air defenses.
- Hours earlier Trump said Putin only hit Poland with "little missiles" by "mistake" (Newsweek, Daily Beast headlines; not opened).

### UK-EU summit in Brussels (Nov 20)
- Fri Nov 20: UK PM Andy Burnham met Ursula von der Leyen and Antonio Costa and agreed deals on British food exports, emissions trading and a Youth Experience Scheme letting 100,000 people under 30 from each side live, work or study in the other for up to three years without existing visa requirements (DW, BBC). Erasmus+ association lets British students join EU universities from 2027. Burnham: a "good day" for Britain and Europe; Treasury says the deal could add up to 0.5% to GDP, up to GBP 17bn a year (BBC; Al Majalla "up to 0.5%"). Burnham says UK will explore even closer ties; question of further steps left open. Dave Keating: EU wants to see a UK election before further talks.
