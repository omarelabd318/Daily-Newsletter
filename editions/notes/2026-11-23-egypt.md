### Madbouly orders a study to regulate the mobile phone market and tie factory incentives to local content
- PM Mostafa Madbouly chaired a meeting Sunday Nov 22 on recent "excessive" phone price rises; directed a government working group to prepare a comprehensive study of the phone market, set mechanisms to control prices and market discipline, and link incentives for manufacturers to the share of local components; aim: affordable devices plus deeper local manufacturing.
- Cabinet spokesman Mohamed Adel: government handles the file on two parallel tracks, support local industry and keep devices affordable. Vivo Egypt (Raghda Amer, PR head) welcomed the move (Al Mal, Nov 22).
- No price figures or deadlines given in the excerpts.

### Finance minister orders study of lifting the tax sukuk yield half a point to 19.5%
- Tax Authority head Rasha Abdel Aal said Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk directed officials (Nov 22) to study raising the yield on the upcoming tax sukuk issue by 0.5 percentage point to 19.5%, "instead of the previously set yield" (implies 19.0%), as an incentive for taxpayers to subscribe.
- Legal basis: Article 115 of the Income Tax Law. Sukuk can settle future tax liabilities. Study only, not a decision.
- Src: Daily News Egypt https://dailynewsegypt.com/2026/11/22/finance-minister-orders-review-of-raising-yield-on-upcoming-tax-sukuk-issuance-to-19-5/ (Zawya copy has same wording: one source)
