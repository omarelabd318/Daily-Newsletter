### COP31 in Antalya ends with no deal
- Ended Sunday Nov 22 after two weeks plus two days of overtime, the longest UN climate summit; first collapse of the main deal since Copenhagen 2009 (BBC).

- Guterres: "I am deeply disappointed that COP31 has ended without consensus on an overarching outcome." Also "There may be no consensus in the negotiating room. There is no ambiguity in the science."
