### Mobile phone prices up about 60% since start of 2026; Madbouly orders market study
- Phone prices up about 60% since the start of the year, per Mohamed Talaat, head of the mobile/telecom division at the Federation of Chambers of Commerce; sales down 25-30% (sector estimates). Causes cited: AI-driven demand for memory chips, shipping, taxes.
- PM Mustafa Madbouly met telecom and industry ministers, regulators and Customs on Sunday Nov 22; ordered a comprehensive market-control study, more local production and local content, incentives tied to local manufacturing.
- Talaat: "the market does not suffer a shortage of supply, but a recession of 25 to 30%". Traders complain of retroactive tax claims (about EGP 3,000 per phone, EGP 20-25k on some iPhones).
