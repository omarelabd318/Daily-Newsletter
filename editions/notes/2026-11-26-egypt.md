### Ring Road fire at Al-Warraq, Giza: 31 dead
- Wed Nov 25 evening (peak traffic): truck carrying petroleum products overturned and caught fire above Tunnel 10, Ring Road, Warraq/Imbaba, Giza; flames spread to passing vehicles. Health Ministry: at least 31 dead, 21 injured; 39 ambulances sent. The National and Sada Elbalad-quoted counts differ on injured (25 in The National/Youm7 earlier; 15 in early Shorouk/Youm7 reports; 30 dead early toll).
- Public Prosecution: preliminary estimate 27 cars and motorcycles burned (Shorouk sources said 33); casualty and damage figures still being assessed. Prosecution initial inspection: incident began with the vehicle swerving, then its load exploded (via Elwatan). Egypt Today called it a "gas-cylinder truck"; all others say petroleum products.
- Prosecutor General Mohamed Shawky ordered urgent probe on cause and responsible parties. PM Madbouly ordered urgent investigation, medical care and compensation (Cabinet spokesperson Mohamed Adel, via Sada Elbalad); no compensation amounts found.
- Transport Ministry denied social-media claim that a fallen power cable caused it; will not comment on cause during probe. Two power circuits disabled, backup supply kept power on (Electricity spokesperson Mansour Abdel Ghany). BRT sent 12 buses from Adly Mansour, cleared stranded commuters in 30 minutes, free service on 75 km stretch (BRT head Reda Saafan). Civil Protection sent 15 fire engines (Youm7).
- Context: CAPMAS road deaths 5,829 in 2025, +10.8% from 5,260 (Zawya/Reuters, BBC "nearly 6,000").
