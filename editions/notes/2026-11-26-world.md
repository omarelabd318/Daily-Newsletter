### US proposes Section 301 tariffs on 14 economies incl. China, India
- USTR, Nov 25: proposed additional tariffs on 14 economies after "structural excess capacity" probes (opened Mar 11, 2026); 14 of 16 probes affirmative.
- Maximum proposed rates: China 20% (alternate 40% if truce commitments unsatisfactory), EU 15% (ends Dec 1, 2028), India 25% (alternate 17% with a deal), Japan 15%, South Korea 15%, Taiwan 15%, Switzerland 15%, Vietnam 35%, Singapore 20%, Indonesia/Malaysia/Thailand/Cambodia 18% or more, Bangladesh 18%. Net of existing duties.
- Legal basis: Section 301, Trade Act of 1974, after Supreme Court struck down Liberation Day tariffs in Feb (emergency powers law). Comments due Jan 5, 2027; hearings Jan 25; earliest effect January. Yale Budget Lab: would restore early-2026 overall rates.
