### Warraq Ring Road fire: toll 31, car driver (not tanker driver) detained 4 days
- Fire Nov 25 on Ring Road above the tunnel in Warraq, Giza: 31 dead; injured 21 (Health Ministry, late Nov 25) later 26 (Youm7, Masry Al Youm, Cabinet/Sada Elbalad "final toll 31 dead, 26 injured"). Early figure was 25 injured.
- Prosecution (Nov 26): arrested the driver of the private car (owner; 21-year-old per Al Quds), detained 4 days pending investigation, sent to forensic medicine incl. drug test. He admitted driving at 110 km/h from Marg towards Haram with his father, weaving between lanes, and hitting the tanker from behind. Inspections: about 30 vehicles hit (DNE; others say 27).
- Forensic medicine finished DNA sampling from victims' families to identify bodies too burned to recognize (Youm7, Nov 26).
- Conflict on mechanism: Al Manassa says the tanker veered, breached the median and overturned; prosecution says private car rammed it. Interior Ministry published CCTV clip Nov 26.
- Politics: Al Manassa (Nov 26) opposition/Civil Democratic Movement demand cabinet resign, name Transport Minister Kamel Al-Wazir; ESDP's Mahmoud Samy filed interpellation to PM and interior, transport, petroleum ministers. CAPMAS: 5,829 road deaths, 84,553 injuries in 2025 (+10.8% and +10.7% on 2024). No government hazardous-truck measure found.
