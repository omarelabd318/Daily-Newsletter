- Same morning Saudi spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki said air defenses destroyed a Houthi cruise missile aimed at Riyadh. The Saudi-led coalition said it began an air operation Fri Nov 27 on Sanaa, Hodeida and Saada.
- Earlier in the week (CNN, Nov 26): projectile hit a Jazan refinery Thu Nov 26, killing one Nepalese worker (Saudi Energy Ministry); Houthis claimed Ras Tanura (Wed Nov 25) and a Riyadh strike that wounded 11 (Saudi authorities). Coalition says 50 targets destroyed Nov 26. Houthi claims of 109 Saudi strikes in 24 hours and 19 civilians killed in 48 hours are unverified (CNN).
- Fighting reignited in July after the 2022 truce.

### US disables a merchant ship it says tried to run the blockade of Iranian ports
- CENTCOM (Fri Nov 27 statement): aircraft fired "precision munitions" at the stern of the Palau-flagged M/V Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman on Thu Nov 26; propulsion disabled, crew unharmed. It had left Bandar Abbas; trackers list Singapore as destination.
- At least the 15th vessel disabled since the blockade began (spring), the sixth since it resumed in July; 158 ships redirected (AP). Previous one Nov 7.
- Iran navy chief Shahram Irani, on state TV Fri: operations "that will not only break the naval blockade but also drive the enemy out of the region's waters."
