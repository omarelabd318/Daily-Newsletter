      Killer Sudoku

      0:00

      How to play. Fill the grid so every row, column and 3 by 3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 once each.

      The dashed shapes are cages: the digits in a cage add up to the number in its corner, and no digit appears twice in a cage. There are no starting digits; the cages are all you need, and there is only one solution.

      Tap a square and type a digit (or use the buttons). Notes lets you pencil in candidates. Arrow keys move.
