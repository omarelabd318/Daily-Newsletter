      Killer Sudoku

      0:00

      How to play. Fill the grid so every row, column and 3 by 3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 once each.

      The dashed shapes are cages: the digits in a cage add up to the number in its corner, and no digit appears twice in a cage. There are no starting digits; the cages are all you need, and there is only one solution.

      Tap a square and type a digit (or use the buttons). Notes lets you pencil in candidates. Arrow keys move.

      The Globe

        Your guesses

          FarBorders it

          How to play. A country is hidden somewhere on the globe. Guess any country and it is painted in: the deeper the red, the closer its border is to the answer's. A country that shares a border turns the darkest red.

          Each guess tells you how far it is, border to border, and whether you are getting warmer or colder. Keep going until you find it; there is no limit.

          Drag to turn the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a painted country for its name. There is one country a day for everyone; Practice hides a fresh one every round, as often as you like.
